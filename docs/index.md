features:
  - title: Gates, not personas
    details: Forty-two active regulators, each a registry record naming the failure it absorbs, where it is enforced, its tests and its stated limitations. A gate without a limitation does not pass the check.
  - title: A small loop that owns execution
    details: Contract, dispatch, verify, route, close. Leases, budgets, worktree isolation and a recovery policy that is data, not code. Nothing reads its own progress off the repository.
  - title: Evidence a host runs
    details: Tests, checks, the inherited suite, the export signature, the protected identity and the glossary are verified by the host at a revision. The model's word is an additional layer, never the only one.
  - title: Obligations with an owner
    details: Every finding, question, proposal and escalation is owed to S3, S5 or a named person, holds the units it names until dispositioned, and is delivered to an outbox with reminders.
  - title: Identity you cannot prompt away
    details: Invariants, glossary and boundaries are files under S5 authority. A unit may propose a change; only a person, checked against the interaction policy, may accept one.
  - title: Measured, not asserted
    details: An eval harness with declared arms, ablation per regulator, repetitions and confidence intervals, so a regulator earns its place in the registry or is retired.
