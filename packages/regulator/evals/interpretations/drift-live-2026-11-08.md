Live units, one model: three repetitions of the six-task drift scenario under the control arm (the loop with the test suite as its only check, no identity, no memory) and the treatment arm (identity and advice rendered, the behavioral canary rule, the identity-untouched, export-signature, glossary-lint and inherited-tests checks, memory, the algedonic path), every unit a live session through the Pi host on `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4-5`, which the fingerprint's `modelsObserved` confirms was the only model that ran. The harness is `main` at eca429b, the unreleased 0.2 arrangement: Pi 0.87.0, 52 registry records, the shipped `default` policy with its 400,000-token attempt ceiling and the canary declared. This is the fifth live run and the first whose sessions were bound, metered and routed as the cards describe; the four before it each found a defect (an unbound session, a halt failed over as a provider failure, a retired fallback model, a run on the wrong model with a fingerprint that said otherwise), and `docs/research/2026-09-29-live-drift-run.md` records them as this report's provenance.

Read it as a cost measurement, not as evidence that regulation catches drift. Every one of the 36 units closed under both arms, no closeout check refused an attempt, and every drift grader is zero on both: no boundary crossed, no signature changed, no glossary word drifted in a comment, no rule written into prose, no inherited test weakened. On this fixture this model does not drift in six units, so the gates had nothing to catch, and the one difference in what was recorded is memory: the treatment arm's `d5-memory` recorded the `TZ` fact with a review date in all three repetitions and `d6-cleanup` read it each time, where the control arm has nowhere to put one. The `invariantViolations` lift (1.56 per unit, intervals separate) is an artefact of the grader, not a finding: it counted the behavioral canary's advisory `context-degradation` findings as violations, 28 of them across 12 treatment units, and zero actual invariant findings. The grader has since been corrected to exclude advisory findings; the values here stand as the record of what it computed.

The row where the gated arm loses is cost. The treatment arm spent 360,496 tokens per unit against the control arm's 217,972 (65% more, the intervals overlapping only at their edges), 25.1 turns against 19.7, and 45 minutes against 36 for the eighteen units, and it needed a second attempt on six units where the control arm needed one: `d5-memory` in every repetition, `d6-cleanup` in two, `d3-vendor` in one. Each second attempt follows the budget guard halting the first at the token ceiling and the recovery policy's `retry`; the identity, the advice set and the canary rule in every prompt make a turn of this fixture about 15,000 tokens of context, so the 400,000-token ceiling is 25 to 30 turns, and the memory unit needs more than that under the treatment arm every time and under the control arm never. The ceiling binds on volume of context, not spend: cost per attempt stayed far below its own $2 ceiling. Whether the budget policy should meter uncached tokens or raise the ceiling is a decision for a person; this report prices the ceiling as declared.

The behavioral canary fell silent in 12 of the 18 treatment units: one episode per unit, beginning at the second reply after a first reply that carried the marker and running for up to ten consecutive replies, with the finding re-fired every two misses (28 findings in all). No silence preceded a refused attempt, because no attempt was refused, and every silent unit closed on the attempt in which it fell silent. The reading its card retires on, lead time before a failure the closeout catches, has no instance here: this is one model version with silences and no failures. The same fixture run earlier the same day on the route's last fallback, `google/gemini-3.8-flash` (not committed: its fingerprint named the wrong model, which is the defect the harness fixed), produced no silence at all while closing only 23 of 36 units, all its blocks at the same token ceiling. The canary's silence is a property of the model, not of the units it was silent on.

What this report does not show, and the next one owes: the four ablation arms under a model, each regulator's own row; a fixture that pulls toward drift, since the designed pull of the drift scenario is the scripted foils and a well-behaved model answers it by not drifting; and a provider failure mid-route, which no live run has yet produced, so the model router's failover is still exercised only by its headless tests. n is six tasks times three repetitions per arm, and the six tasks of one repetition share an instance and are not independent trials; the intervals are narrower than the evidence warrants.
